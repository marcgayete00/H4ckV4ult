rsync (TCP/873)

rsync (remote sync) is a utility for transferring and synchronizing files between a computer and a storage drive and across networked computers by comparing the modification times and sizes of files

Usage

    Accessing shares

      nc -nv 127.0.0.1 873

      Once insede you must type #list in order to list all shares

      In case we would like to check what content is inside a share we could perform

      rsync -av --list-only rsync://127.0.0.1/$sharename

      There is also another way of obtaining the shares through nmap

      nmap -p 873 --script rsync-list-modules

    Transferring files

      rsync -av FILE_TO_TRANSFER rsync://192.168.1.100/SHARE/

      you could do the same with folders

      rsync -av DIRECTORY_TO_TRANSFER rsync://192.168.1.100/SHARE/

      In order to download all files from a share you could

      rsync -av rsync://10.10.10.20/backup/ ./backup/

      In case of needing a username and password all previous commands would need to be modified to include them. Example:

      rsync -av user@10.10.10.20:/var/www/html ./
